Exoplanet Classification Guide

Understanding the diverse worlds beyond our solar system

circle

Gas Giant

Like Jupiter or Saturn

Massive planets composed mostly of hydrogen and helium. These giants often orbit very close to their stars ("Hot Jupiters") but can be found far out as well.

public

Neptune-like

Like Neptune or Uranus

Gaseous worlds smaller than Jupiter but larger than Earth. They often have thick atmospheres of hydrogen, helium, and methane, giving them a blue hue.

landscape

Super-Earth

Rocky but Massive

Planets more massive than Earth but lighter than Neptune. They might be rocky, ocean worlds, or have thin atmospheres. A common type not found in our solar system.

terrain

Terrestrial

Earth-sized & Rocky

Small, rocky worlds with iron-rich cores. These are the prime targets in the search for life, especially if found in the habitable zone.
